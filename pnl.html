Trading Calendar

How this is computed. Every BUY the desk published is replayed as a paper trade filled at the next session's open, weighted exactly as the allocator sized it, and marked to the close each session thereafter. Nothing is sold: the system is a dollar-cost accumulator, and protected names are never exited.
  • The book is scaled so total capital deployed over the whole window equals a notional $10,000. Percentages are unchanged; no real account size, NAV or position is shown.
  • A day's P&L is the mark-to-market change of every open paper position that session. Win% is the share of those positions that closed green.
  • Days marked PENDING carry a published signal with no settled price yet.
  • Gaps in the price cache are carried forward, so a stale name contributes $0 rather than a phantom move.
Hypothetical results from published signals. Not investment advice, and not a record of executed trades.
